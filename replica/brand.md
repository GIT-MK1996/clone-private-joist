## Name

**Klusnota** ("klus" and "nota"): says what it does, short, easy to spell after hearing it once, no awkward meaning in Dutch, English or German. Not similar to Joist in sound, look or meaning.

Rejected: Vaknota (too close to the existing product Vaktuur), Vastbon / Klaarbon / Duimbon (the "-bon" ending sits too close to the competitors Vakbon and Werkbon).

- Mark: rounded yellow square, ruler ticks along the top, dark check mark. Wordmark: "klusnota" in lowercase, extra bold.

## Voice

**Nuchter, direct, behulpzaam.**
- Nuchter, not boring: plain words, no hype.
- Direct, not curt: say what happens, in short sentences.
- Behulpzaam, not childish: explain what to do next, no exclamation marks everywhere.

| do | don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Je offerte de deur uit vóór je weer in de bus zit." | "Revolutionair offerteplatform!" |
| "Stuur hem via WhatsApp." | "Deel je document via diverse kanalen." |
| "Geen limiet op het aantal facturen." | "Flexibele pakketten op maat." |
| "Vraag? Stuur een WhatsApp." | "Neem contact op met onze supportafdeling." |
